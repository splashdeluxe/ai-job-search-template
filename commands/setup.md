# /setup

Goal: turn the blank templates into the owner's approved system. Ask, do not guess.

## Path A: from documents (try this first)
If the owner has a documents folder (current CV, LinkedIn export, diplomas, reference letters, past applications), read it and draft the profile from what is there. Show the owner what came from which document. Fill gaps with questions, never with guesses. Re-running Path A after adding documents is safe.

## Path B: interview
1. Read `01-candidate-profile.md`, `00-tracks.md` and `02-master-facts-sheet.md`. List every TODO.
2. Ask for the owner's current CV and LinkedIn, then ask the TODO questions in small groups: target, geography, rights, history, numbers, salary.
3. Write the answers into the three files exactly as stated. Unknown numbers go to the "does not know" list.
4. Show the owner the three files in full. Fix what they correct. Nothing is final until they approve.
5. Build the starter ATS watchlist in `04-sources.md` from the companies the owner names plus direct competitors in their tracks, and adapt the portal list to the owner's market.

## Voice sample
6. Ask for one short sample of the owner's own writing (a mail, a note, a post). Save its traits (plain or formal, sentence length, words they use) as the voice target for `05-writing-check.md`.

## Dispatcher
7. Ask: "Should Muse run this for you on a schedule?" If yes, fill `06-dispatcher.md` together: shortlist time, briefing time, dispatch interval, timezone, quiet hours, approval mode. Then create the scheduled jobs in Muse and show the owner the list of jobs created.

## Dashboard
8. Ask: "Dashboard, or dashboard plus world map?" Build the dashboard in Muse to `dashboard/DASHBOARD-SPEC.md` from the tracker and shortlist, and show it to the owner before the pilot.

## Working files
8b. Create the two local files the system writes to, if they are missing: copy `tracker/tracker-template.csv` to `tracker/job_search_tracker.csv`, and create `seen_jobs.json` as an empty list. Both stay local and gitignored.

## Close
9. End with a summary: tracks and weights, accepted geographies, rights per geography, salary anchors, what is still unknown, which jobs are scheduled, and the pilot as the next step.
