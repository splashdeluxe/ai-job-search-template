# /upskill

Goal: turn the search's own evidence into a short learning plan. No generic course lists.

1. Collect the gaps: every gap line from /rank and /apply briefs, and every stated rejection reason from /outcome, grouped by how often each one cost a role.
2. Rank the gaps by damage: how many roles they blocked, and whether those roles were ones the owner wanted.
3. For the top gaps, propose one small closing action each: a project with a visible output, past work to surface and document, or a short course with a named provider and a time estimate. One action per gap. Five gaps at most.
4. The owner picks what to do. Picks are recorded with a date, and when the work is done, the profile is updated with the new evidence, so the next /rank scores it.
5. Report the plan in the briefing shape: the gap, the cost so far, the action, the expected change in fit.
