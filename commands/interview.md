# /interview

Goal: prep from evidence, not from hope.

1. Load the saved job description and the requirement-to-evidence table from the /apply prep pack.
2. List the likely questions per requirement, plus the gaps the panel is most likely to probe.
3. Draft answers from the owner's real record only: situation, what they did, the number where they have one. Mark any answer that leans on a bridge (adjacent work) as a bridge.
4. Prepare the owner's questions for them: team, reporting line, why the role is open, how success is measured in 90 days, visa and relocation support where relevant.
5. Save the prep in `applications/` next to the role's pack, with the date.
