# Tracks

Status: TEMPLATE. The owner sets these in /setup. One main track, at most two side tracks.

## How scoring works
- Each posting is scored per track on four dimensions, 0 to 100: technical fit, experience fit, behavioural fit, career fit.
- Before scoring, two hard gates run: eligibility (location and work rights, from the facts sheet) and language (the posting's required languages vs the owner's honest levels). A failed gate stops the role. It is not scored down and quietly kept.
- Best track wins. Tracks within 5 points of each other are both reported.
- Weights below are starting points. The owner can change them in /setup.
