# Setup

## On GitHub (recommended)
1. Click **Use this template** and create your copy. Set it to **Private**. A public fork stays public on GitHub, and setup writes personal data into tracked files, so the template route is the safe one.
2. Clone it to a folder you will keep.
3. Open that folder with Muse (join via the README's free section) and say: "Read `AGENTS.md` and run `commands/setup.md` with me."

## Without GitHub
1. Download the zip and unzip it to a folder you will keep.
2. Same instruction to Muse.

## What setup needs from you
- Your documents, if you have them: current CV, LinkedIn export, diplomas, reference letters, past applications. Setup reads them first and drafts your profile from evidence.
- Or the interview: target roles (one main track, at most two side tracks), geography, and your work rights per place, stated exactly. Your agent will push back if an answer sounds optimistic, that is the point.
- Languages at honest levels, education, tools, and your best hard numbers.
- Numbers you do not know. They get written down once and never asked again.
- One short writing sample of your own, as the voice target for the writing check.

## What setup asks at the end
- **Dispatcher:** should Muse run sourcing, applying, a morning shortlist and a daily briefing on a schedule? You pick the times, the interval and the approval mode. Settings land in `06-dispatcher.md`.
- **Dashboard:** dashboard only, or dashboard plus a world map of your applications? Built in your Muse from `dashboard/DASHBOARD-SPEC.md`.

## After setup
- Approve the profile, tracks and facts sheet in full.
- Your agent creates two local working files on first run: `tracker/job_search_tracker.csv` (copied from `tracker/tracker-template.csv`) and `seen_jobs.json`. Both are gitignored and stay on your machine.
- Connect your mail and Drive if you want confirmations tracked and CVs filed. Logins go in Muse's secure vault, never in this repo or in chat.
- Run one pilot application end to end. Then the dispatcher takes over.

## Staying safe
Your copy holds sensitive facts. Keep the GitHub repo private, keep the live tracker local (it is gitignored), and read `PRIVACY.md` and `SECURITY.md`.
